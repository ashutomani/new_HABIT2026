About the conference

Where health and agriculture meet in the lab

HABIT-2026 is the second edition of the International Conference on Health and Agricultural Biotechnology: Interdisciplinary Trends, organized by the Department of Biotechnology at Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT) Allahabad, in Prayagraj — one of India's oldest and most culturally significant cities.

The conference brings together researchers from across the globe to examine recent advances at the intersection of health, agriculture and allied disciplines, with sessions led by domain experts and a dedicated platform for young scientists to present their work and compete for the HABIT Young Scientist Awards.

Its interdisciplinary scope — spanning medical biotechnology, agricultural science, bioinformatics, environmental biotechnology and nanotechnology — makes it relevant to academic researchers, PhD scholars, and industry professionals alike.

Scope

Conference themes

Papers and abstracts are invited across the following thrust areas. This list is illustrative — interdisciplinary and cross-cutting submissions are welcome.

Health & Medical Biotechnology

Diagnostics, therapeutics and translational research addressing human health.

Agricultural Biotechnology

Crop improvement, plant science and sustainable agricultural innovation.

Bioinformatics & Computational Biology

Computational chemistry, data-driven biology and AI applications in the life sciences.

Environmental Biotechnology

Climate-resilient approaches and biotechnological solutions for environmental challenges.

Molecular Biology & Nanotechnology

Genomics, proteomics and nanobiotechnology at the molecular scale.

Interdisciplinary Approaches

Cross-cutting research spanning health, food, agriculture and environmental science.
